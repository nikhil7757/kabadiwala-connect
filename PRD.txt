1. Overview

1.1 Official problem (summary)

Most of India’s end-of-life electronics passes through informal scrap
dealers, waste-pickers and local aggregators, who have last-mile reach
and low collection cost but sit outside the formal recycling system
created under the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 (EPR framework with
authorized recyclers). The gap is informational and institutional:
collectors do not know fair prices, which nearby recyclers are
authorized, how to complete a compliant handover, or how to get a record
of the transaction. Material then goes to unsafe backyard processing
(open cable burning, acid leaching of circuit boards, manual
desoldering), which harms workers and loses valuable materials such as
lithium, cobalt, neodymium, tantalum, gallium and indium.

1.2 What we build

A vernacular, low-literacy, offline-tolerant platform with three
faces: 1. Collector app (installable PWA, Android Chrome, Hindi,
Marathi, English) to photograph and list lots, see fair prices, pick a
nearby authorized buyer, complete a traceable handover and track
earnings and safety guidance. 2. Recycler panel (web) to keep
authorization and rates current, quote lots, confirm handovers and
record payments. 3. Admin page (web) to verify or suspend recyclers,
review anomaly flags and export anonymized datasets.

1.3 Principles

-   Formal channel must be economically attractive and convenient, not
    an added compliance burden: three taps to a price, no typing, cash
    always allowed, digital payment optional.
-   Offline first: every collector action except the very first login
    works without internet.
-   Small and cheap: entry-level Android, small download, low memory.
-   Honest data: datasets are generated by real operations, validated,
    updated and used by the app, not a static file.
-   Minimal personal data: the collector profile holds a phone number,
    language and general area.

Authoritative list, versions policy and forbidden items are in TRD
Sections 1 to 3. Summary: npm workspaces monorepo, TypeScript strict;
web is React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS 4, React Router 7, Dexie
(IndexedDB), zustand, i18next, TensorFlow.js, vite-plugin-pwa; server is

5. Functional requirements

F-1 Onboarding and login. Language chooser (hi, mr, en) with audio
greeting; phone plus 6-digit code; first login needs network; token
stored in IndexedDB; initial sync of reference data.

F-2 Lots. Take 1 to 4 photos (compressed, hashed), choose material by
icon (optional on-device AI suggestion), enter weight and condition, see
an instant estimate from cached prices, save offline, GPS captured at
save with fallback. Hazardous material (battery, CRT) shows the safety
popup first.

F-3 Price board. Per district: latest buying rate per category, unit,
trend arrow, market range, last-updated date, stale warning after 7
days, 30-day sparkline, spoken price.

F-4 Recycler matching. Top 3 eligible recyclers ranked by rate,
distance, pickup, reliability and freshness; badges; choose with
optional pickup; works offline from the cached directory; server
confirms.

F-5 Handover. Collector declares weight and location, gets a handover
reference, QR and 6-digit code from the server; recycler scans or enters
it and the verified weight; both records feed a hash-chained trace.

F-6 Ledger and payment. Recycler records amount and mode (cash or UPI);
collector sees Received and Pending; partial payments supported; digital
payment is never required.

F-7 Safety. Six pictogram cards with audio (burning, CRT, batteries,
acid, gloves, children); popup before hazardous lots.

F-8 Sync. Outbox of actions, automatic sync when online, visible pending
and rejected items, idempotent replay.

F-9 Recycler panel. Dashboard, rates, incoming lots, quote, handover
confirm, payment, profile. Blocked for unverified recyclers except
viewing the profile.

F-10 Admin. Verify or suspend recyclers, add recyclers, anomaly flags
with trace verification, dataset stats and CSV exports.

F-11 Settings. Language, district, operating area, text size, audio on
or off, About with SAMPLE DATA notice, clear local data, logout.

8.3 Price history

For every district and category generate 60 days of daily buyingPrice
(source SEED, isSampleData) from a fixed-seed pseudo-random generator so
runs are reproducible: base rate times a factor with drift plus small
noise (about plus or minus 4 percent). Make PCB in Pune drift upward and
CABLE in Nagpur drift downward so the trend arrows show both directions.
marketMin is 0.90 times the price and marketMax is 1.15 times.

11. Offline-first requirements

1.  After one online visit (install), the collector app loads and every
    screen in APP_FLOW marked “Works offline” works with airplane mode
    on.
2.  Lot creation makes zero network requests. Reference data comes from
    Dexie.
3.  Actions go to the outbox and replay in order. Replays are idempotent
    (clientId, actionId).
4.  Handover code needs the server once: the collector queues
    HANDOVER_INITIATE offline and gets the code on the next sync
    (APP_FLOW 5.6). The screen says so in pictogram plus audio.
5.  Stale data is labelled (rates older than 7 days).
6.  The Playwright offline test (Section 15, test 1 and 2) is mandatory:
    block all external requests, first visit online, go offline, create
    a lot, go online, verify sync and that the server has the lot with a
    KC-MH-... reference.
7.  Size and memory: precache only the app shell and the collector
    chunk; recycler, admin, recharts and the ML model are lazy; measured
    sizes go in DECISIONS.md against TRD 15 targets.

12. Algorithms

Full definitions in TRD Section 11. Extra detail needed to implement
them without guessing:

12.1 Matching score (weights in matching.config.ts)

Eligible: VERIFIED, accepts the category, distanceKm <= serviceRadiusKm.
For each eligible recycler compute, each in the range 0 to 1: -
rateScore = offeredRate / highestOfferedRateAmongEligible -
distanceScore = 1 - min(distanceKm, serviceRadiusKm) / serviceRadiusKm -
pickupScore = pickupAvailable ? 1 : 0 - reliabilityScore = confirmed
handovers divided by lots selected, last 90 days; 0.5 when fewer than 3
lots - freshnessScore = 1 if the rate is at most 1 day old, falling
linearly to 0 at 14 days

score = 40*rateScore + 25*distanceScore + 15*pickupScore + 10*reliabilityScore + 10*freshnessScore
(0 to 100, one decimal). Sort by score descending; ties by lower
distance. Badges: AUTHORIZED (all), PICKUP, BEST_RATE (highest rate),
NEAREST (smallest distance). Return 3 by default, 5 maximum. Suspended
and pending recyclers never appear.

12.2 Valuation

round2(weightKg * rate) (TRD 11.1). The estimate screen shows the rate
date and, when the state average was used, the fallback label.

12.3 Anomaly detection

TRD 11.3. Reference set is the previous 30 days of PriceEntry for the
same category and city. Nothing blocks a transaction.

12.4 Price trend

TRD 11.4. Minimum 3 daily averages, else no trend.

18. Out of scope, assumptions, known differences, open questions

Out of scope for the prototype: real SMS OTP, real UPI or payment
gateway, MinIO or cloud storage, blockchain (the hash chain is a
lightweight tamper-evidence, not a blockchain), native Android app, iOS,
push notifications, recycler self-registration, multi-state expansion,
real government authorization API integration, payments processing, EPR
certificate issuance.

Assumptions: the prototype ships as an installable PWA for Chrome on
Android (small, no store friction, works offline through a service
worker); authorization details in the seed are fictional; the
prototype’s district list is Maharashtra only.

Known differences and overrides: 1. Kickoff prompt mentioned MinIO; TRD
Section 20 defers it. Use local disk. 2. API prefix is /api/v1 (TRD),
not /api. 3. Recycler ranking runs on the client from the cached
directory and is confirmed by the server (TRD 20). 4. The handover code
is derived from the handover reference and is not stored (BACKEND.md
6.2). 5. Schema additions beyond the TRD field list are in BACKEND.md
3.2.

Open questions for the team (the agent must not answer these): real
district list beyond Maharashtra, real recycler partners for a pilot,
who records the Hindi and Marathi audio, who trains the material
classifier and with which photos, how unit economics numbers will be
collected.
