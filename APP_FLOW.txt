Kabadiwala Connect: App Flow

Every page, route, navigation rule and state transition for the
prototype. Companion to PRD.md, TRD.md and UI_UX.md. Do not add pages or
routes that are not listed here.

3. Route guards (evaluated in this order)

1.  Language guard (collector routes only). If no language saved in
    meta, redirect to /welcome.
2.  Auth guard. No valid token for the required role: collector routes
    go to /login; /recycler/* goes to /recycler/login; /admin/* goes to
    /admin/login.
3.  Role guard. A logged-in user with the wrong role sees a Forbidden
    screen with a button to their own home.
4.  Token expiry. A 401 from the server during sync clears the token and
    redirects to the login screen; offline app use continues to work
    until the next sync attempt.
5.  Lot guard. /lot/:clientId* with an unknown clientId shows “Lot not
    found” with a button to /lots.
6.  Flow guard for new lot steps. /lot/new/material requires a photo in
    the draft; /lot/new/weight requires a material; /lot/new/estimate
    requires weight. Otherwise redirect to the earliest missing step.
    The draft lives in Dexie (lots with status: "LOCAL_DRAFT") so it
    survives app close.

4. Global navigation rules

-   The collector app has no bottom tab bar. Navigation is home tiles
    plus a top bar with a Back arrow (left) and a language chip and
    connectivity dot (right). Every screen has one primary action in a
    sticky bottom bar.
-   Android hardware Back goes to the previous route; from /home it
    exits the app. On the new-lot steps, Back goes to the previous step
    and keeps the draft.
-   Home icon in the top bar returns to /home from any deep screen.
-   Recycler and admin use a left sidebar (collapsible on narrow
    screens) and a top bar with user, logout and connectivity.
-   Logout (all roles) clears the token; the collector’s local data
    stays on the device unless “Clear local data” is chosen in Settings
    (with confirmation).

5. Collector flows

5.1 First launch and login

    flowchart TD
      A[Open app] --> B{Language saved?}
      B -- No --> C[/welcome: choose Hindi, Marathi or English/]
      C --> D[Audio greeting plays]
      D --> E[/login: enter phone number/]
      B -- Yes --> F{Token valid?}
      F -- No --> E
      F -- Yes --> H[/home/]
      E --> G[/otp: enter 6-digit code/]
      G -- Correct --> I[Initial sync: materials, prices, recyclers, safety]
      I --> H
      G -- Wrong --> G2[Pictorial + audio error, retry]
      G2 --> G
      E -- Offline --> E2[Message: internet needed for first login only]

Details: - /welcome: three large buttons (हिन्दी, मराठी, English).
Tapping one saves the language, plays the greeting audio, and goes to
/login. - /login: big number pad, 10-digit phone. Primary action “Send
code”. Offline shows the message above and disables the button. - /otp:
six big boxes, number pad. Demo hint chip shows the demo code when
DEMO_MODE is on (SAMPLE label). Success triggers initial sync with a
progress bar, then /home.

5.2 Home

Four large tiles in a 2 x 2 grid: New Lot (/lot/new/photo), Price Board
(/prices), My Lots (/lots), Earnings (/earnings). Below: a wide Safety
button (/safety) and a small Sync chip (/sync) showing pending count.
Top bar: settings gear (/settings), language chip, connectivity dot. A
ticker strip shows 3 to 4 rates from the price board (static text if
reduced motion).

5.3 Create a lot

    flowchart TD
      H[/home/] -->|New Lot| P[/lot/new/photo/]
      P -->|Photo taken, 1 to 4| M[/lot/new/material/]
      M -->|Material chosen| S{Hazardous category?}
      S -- Battery or CRT --> SP[Safety popup + audio]
      SP --> W[/lot/new/weight/]
      S -- No --> W
      W -->|Weight and condition set| E[/lot/new/estimate/]
      E -->|Save lot| L[Lot saved locally, outbox action LOT_CREATE]
      L --> MT[/lot/:clientId/match/]
      MT -->|Choose recycler| D[/lot/:clientId/]
      E -->|Save without choosing| D

Details per step: - Photo (/lot/new/photo): camera button opens the
camera; up to 4 photos as thumbnails; delete on long press; primary
“Next” enabled with at least 1 photo. While on this screen the ML model
preloads in the background. - Material (/lot/new/material): grid of
large material icons with names. If the model is available, the top
suggestion is highlighted with its confidence and a speaker button says
the name. Message “not sure, please choose” if confidence is below 0.60.
Choosing any icon proceeds. Hazardous categories (BATTERY, CRT) open the
safety popup before continuing; the collector must tap “I understand”
(green tick) to close it. - Weight (/lot/new/weight): big number pad
plus a slider for quick input; unit kg (or count for piece-based
categories); condition chips (Working, Broken, Burnt) with icons.
Primary “See value”. - Estimate (/lot/new/estimate): huge rupee amount,
“rates last updated ” line, speaker button that reads the amount, and a
fallback label if a state-level average was used. Primary “Find buyer”
saves the lot (GPS captured now) and goes to match. Secondary “Save for
later” saves and goes to /lot/:clientId.

5.4 Match and select recycler (/lot/:clientId/match)

-   Shows the top 3 ranked authorized recyclers as cards: name,
    distance, offered rate for this material, badges (Authorized,
    Pickup, Best rate, Nearest), estimated value at that rate.
-   Actions on a card: Choose (with a Pickup toggle if the recycler
    offers pickup). Choosing queues SELECT_RECYCLER, sets local status
    LISTED, and goes to /lot/:clientId.
-   Empty state: “No authorized buyer near you for this material yet”
    with a Back button and a note that the lot is saved.
-   The list comes from the cached directory; a “Refresh” chip appears
    when online.

5.5 Lot detail (/lot/:clientId)

Shows photos, material, weight, estimated value, chosen recycler, quote
(if any), and a vertical timeline of statuses: Saved, Buyer chosen,
Quote received, Quote accepted, Handover, Confirmed, Paid. Primary
action changes with status:

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Status                  Primary action          Goes to
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -------------------------
  DRAFT (saved, no buyer) Find buyer              /lot/:clientId/match

  LISTED (waiting for     Waiting (disabled) with none
  quote)                  Sync button             

  QUOTED                  Accept quote            queues ACCEPT_QUOTE

  ACCEPTED                Start handover          /lot/:clientId/handover

  HANDED_OVER             Show code again         /lot/:clientId/handover

  CONFIRMED / PAID        View record             expands trace summary
                                                  (lot ID, hash,
                                                  timestamps)

  Any before ACCEPTED     Cancel lot (secondary)  confirm dialog then
                                                  CANCEL_LOT
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.6 Handover

    sequenceDiagram
      participant C as Collector app
      participant S as Server
      participant R as Recycler panel
      C->>C: Weigh lot, tap Start handover
      C->>C: Queue HANDOVER_INITIATE (works offline)
      C->>S: Sync (when online)
      S-->>C: handoverRef, OTP, QR payload
      C->>C: Show QR and 6-digit code
      R->>R: Scan QR or type code, enter verified weight
      R->>S: POST /handover/confirm
      S-->>R: Confirmed (lot CONFIRMED)
      R->>S: POST /ledger (payment)
      S-->>C: Next sync: status CONFIRMED and ledger entry

Collector screen /lot/:clientId/handover: weight confirmation, photo of
the lot at handover (optional additional photo), GPS captured, then
Start handover creates the queued action. After sync the screen shows
the QR (with notch-corner frame), the 6-digit code in large digits, and
the handover reference. If the app is offline, it shows “Connect once to
get your code” (the code is issued by the server). After the recycler
confirms, the next sync moves the lot to CONFIRMED and a green tick
screen appears.

5.7 Price board (/prices)

List of categories with rate, unit, trend arrow (up, down, flat), market
range, and a speaker button per row; a district selector chip at the
top; “Last updated” line; if data is older than 7 days a “rates may be
old” warning chip. Tapping a row expands a 30-day sparkline. Offline
uses cached data.

5.8 Earnings (/earnings)

Two summary cards: Received and Pending. Below, a list of entries: lot
code, material icon, amount, mode (cash or UPI icon), status chip.
Tapping an entry opens /lot/:clientId. Empty state: “No earnings yet”.

5.9 Safety (/safety, /safety/:code)

Grid of safety cards with pictograms (no burning cables, do not open
CRT, safe batteries, no acid on PCBs, wear gloves, keep away from
children). Tapping opens the card detail: large pictogram, 2 to 3 short
lines, speaker button. Cards flip on tap (front pictogram, back
instructions) and the detail route is the deep link to the same content.

5.10 Sync (/sync)

Shows online or offline state, lastSyncAt, pending action count with a
list (type, lot code, time, status), REJECTED actions with a
plain-language reason and a Retry or Dismiss button, and a big Sync now
button.

5.11 Settings (/settings)

Language, district and operating area (chips), text size (Normal,
Large), audio on or off, About (version, “SAMPLE DATA” notice,
field-note link), Clear local data (confirmation dialog), Logout.

6. Offline and connectivity behaviour

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Situation                           Behaviour
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  Offline                             Orange dot and banner “Offline:
                                      your work is saved on this phone”
                                      (audio available). All lot
                                      creation, price board, ledger
                                      reading, safety and handover
                                      initiation work

  Back online                         Green dot; automatic sync; toast
                                      “Synced” with count

  Sync failure                        Banner “Could not sync, will retry”
                                      with a Sync now button

  Stale rates (older than 7 days)     Warning chip on estimate and price
                                      board

  Server rejects an action            Card on /sync and a red dot on the
                                      lot; nothing is deleted silently
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Recycler flows

    flowchart TD
      L[/recycler/login/] --> D[/recycler dashboard/]
      D --> R[/recycler/rates: set rates/]
      D --> LS[/recycler/lots: incoming lots/]
      LS --> LD[/recycler/lots/:id/]
      LD -->|Send quote| LD
      D --> H[/recycler/handover/]
      H -->|Scan or enter code + verified weight| C[Confirmed]
      C --> P[Record payment: cash or UPI, paid amount]
      D --> PR[/recycler/profile/]

-   Dashboard: counts (new lots, quotes waiting, handovers to confirm,
    payments pending), latest 5 lots, own current rates with freshness,
    verification status banner (Verified, Pending, Suspended).
-   Rates: table with one row per accepted material; edit inline; Save
    writes rates and price entries. Warning if rates are older than 7
    days.
-   Incoming lots: filterable list (status, material); rows show photo,
    material, weight, estimated value, distance, collector area.
-   Lot detail: photos, details, estimated value, quote input (prefilled
    with rate x weight), “Send quote”. After quote the lot shows
    “Waiting for collector”.
-   Handover confirm: camera scanner and manual fields (handover
    reference plus 6-digit code), verified weight input,
    weight-difference warning. On success: summary, trace hash, and a
    payment form (amount paid, cash or UPI, status). Payment sets ledger
    entry; when fully paid, lot is PAID.
-   Profile: name, phone, location on a simple lat/lng form with “Use my
    location”, service radius, pickup toggle, accepted materials list
    (read only, edited via rates), authorization details (read only,
    edited by admin).
-   Unverified state: if status is Pending or Suspended, the recycler
    can log in and see the profile but cannot set rates or quote; a
    banner explains why.

8. Admin flows

-   Recyclers: table with authorization number, issuing body, valid
    till, status. Actions: Verify, Suspend, Add recycler (dialog). A
    verified recycler immediately becomes visible in collector
    directories after their next sync.
-   Flags: list of lots with anomaly reason and the expected range;
    “Open trace” shows the hash chain and verification result.
-   Data: dataset cards (materials, prices, recyclers, transactions,
    traceability, collectors) each with a CSV download button and row
    counts; stats cards (lots by status, lots per day chart, verified
    recyclers).

9. Screen states every screen must implement

Loading (skeleton, not spinner alone), empty (icon plus one line plus
audio), error (pictogram plus one line plus retry), offline (banner,
plus disabled actions that need network), success (green tick and short
audio cue for key actions: lot saved, quote accepted, handover
confirmed).
