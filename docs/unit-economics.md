## 2. Platform Sustainability Hypotheses (EPR Financing)

Under the **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022**, electrical and electronic equipment producers possess Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) targets:

1. **EPR Credit Facilitation**: Authorized recyclers purchase informal scrap with digital, verifiable source traces (hash-chain trace records). Recyclers generate certified EPR credits. The platform levies a micro-service transaction fee (0.5%–1.5%) on recyclers per confirmed transaction.
2. **Collector Incentives**: Formalized collectors channel hazardous materials (CRT lead glass, Lithium-ion) away from open fires and acid dumps, qualifying for municipal clean-energy and ESG transition subsidies.
3. **No Collector Subscription Fees**: The collector application remains 100% free with zero paywalls, ensuring zero friction for adoption among informal waste-pickers.
