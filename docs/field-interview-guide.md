# Field Interview & Usability Guide

> **Protocol**: Grounded field engagement with informal e-waste collectors and aggregators under ethical guidelines (no personal identifiable data published, consent obtained prior to demonstration).

---

## 1. Ethical Guidelines & Informed Consent

1. **Anonymity**: Never collect or record the collector's full name, residential address, or Aadhaar/ID numbers. Only record general operating district/area.
2. **Photography**: Do not photograph faces. Photograph only materials, scales, and mobile screens with explicit verbal consent.
3. **No Coercion**: Clearly explain that the platform is an informational tool and does not impose taxation or administrative reporting.
4. **Compensation**: Honor the collector's working time appropriately if field interviews interrupt business operations.

---

## 2. Interview Script (Vernacular Hindi / Marathi Prompts)

### Introduction
- *Hindi*: "नमस्ते भाई साहब। हम एक मोबाइल ऐप का परीक्षण कर रहे हैं जो कबाड़ी भाइयों को ई-कचरे (पुराने तार, सर्किट बोर्ड, बैटरी) का सही बाज़ार भाव जानने और अधिकृत खरीदारों से जुड़ने में मदद करता है।"
- *Marathi*: "नमस्कार. आम्ही एका मोबाइल ॲपची चाचणी घेत आहोत जे भंगार गोळा करणाऱ्या बांधवांना ई-कचऱ्याचे (तारा, सर्किट बोर्ड, बॅटरी) योग्य दर मिळवून देण्यासाठी आणि अधिकृत खरेदीदारांशी जोडण्यासाठी तयार केले आहे."

### Discovery Questions
1. How do you find out current buying rates for electronics scrap?
2. When selling to aggregators, how are weights and payments verified?
3. Have you ever attempted to sell directly to an authorized recycling facility? What was the outcome?
4. What safety precautions are taken when dismantling or storing hazardous items like batteries or CRT monitors?

---

## 3. Usability Task Protocol

Guide the collector through the following flow without touching their phone screen:
1. **Language & Greeting**: Let them choose Hindi or Marathi on the welcome screen.
2. **Rate Discovery**: Ask them to check today's rate for PCB or Cable on the Price Board.
3. **Lot Entry**: Ask them to enter 10 kg of broken circuit boards. Observe if the numeric keypad or weight slider is preferred.
4. **Offline Mode**: Turn on airplane mode and ask them to complete saving the lot. Check for understanding of the offline banner.
5. **Handover**: Demonstrate the derived 6-digit code and QR code. Ask if they feel confident showing this code to a buyer.
